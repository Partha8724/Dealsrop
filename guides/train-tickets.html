Cheapest Way to Book Train Tickets in India

Unlike flights, Indian train fares are fixed by Indian Railways. The ticket price is the same on IRCTC and on every travel app. What changes is the extra fees you pay on top.

What you actually pay

  • Base fare – set by Indian Railways, identical everywhere.
  • IRCTC convenience fee – charged on online e-tickets. At the time of writing it is ₹15 + GST for Sleeper/non-AC and ₹30 + GST for AC classes, with lower charges for UPI payments. It is non-refundable.
  • App service fee – authorised apps may add their own charge on top.
  • Payment gateway charges – depend on how you pay. UPI is usually the cheapest, and some apps run ₹0 gateway-fee offers on UPI.

Fees change often. Always check the final amount on the payment page before you pay.

How to pay the least

  1. Book directly on IRCTC (website or Rail Connect app) to avoid extra app fees.
  2. Pay with UPI to keep gateway charges low.
  3. If you use a travel app, compare its final price with IRCTC and look for cashback offers.
  4. Book early. Cheaper classes and quota seats sell out first.

Tatkal booking timings

  • AC classes: booking opens at 10:00 AM, one day before the journey (not counting the journey day).
  • Non-AC classes (Sleeper, 2S): booking opens at 11:00 AM, one day before the journey.

Tatkal tickets cost more than normal tickets, so book a regular ticket early whenever you can.

Quick tips

  • Save passenger details in your IRCTC master list to book faster.
  • Keep your IRCTC account verified and log in a few minutes early for Tatkal.
  • If you're waitlisted, check your PNR status regularly. Tickets that stay fully waitlisted are cancelled automatically, and the fare is refunded under railway rules.

This guide is for information only. Always confirm current rules on the official IRCTC website.
