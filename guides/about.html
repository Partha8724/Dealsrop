About DealDrop India

DealDrop India checks product prices and flight fares automatically every few hours. It keeps a full price history, so you can see whether today's "deal" is really a good price or just a normal one.

How it works

  1. Prices are collected from official store and travel data feeds.
  2. Every price is saved, building a history for each product.
  3. We compare today's price with the last 30 days and highlight real drops.
